Roof Repair in Omaha, NE: Costs and What to Expect

Shingles blown off, a leak around the chimney, or hail damage after a storm? Here is what roof repairs typically cost, what changes the price, how to tell whether a quote is fair, and how to get a local Omaha roofer out to your home.

What roof repair costs in Omaha

Most roof repairs cost a few hundred dollars, and very few run past a few thousand. Nationally, homeowners pay an average of $531 for a roof repair, according to This Old House's roof repair cost guide (updated March 2026). No public source publishes Omaha-only repair prices, so treat the national figures below as a starting point. Only a roofer who has seen your roof can give you a real price.

Costs for the repairs Omaha roofs need most

National ranges from the same This Old House guide, for the problems that come up most on Omaha homes:

  • Shingle damage: $50 to $800. Wind-lifted or missing shingles after a storm.
  • Holes and leaks: $100 to $1,500. More if water has soaked the underlayment or the wood beneath it.
  • Chimney or roof flashing: $200 to $500. Brick chimneys on older Omaha homes are a classic leak point.
  • Roof vents: $75 to $250. Cracked vent covers and dried-out vent boots.
  • Roof valley: $300 to $1,000. Where two slopes meet and water runs heaviest.
  • Hail damage: $700 to $4,000. Bigger hail jobs often become an insurance claim. See our hail damage claim guide.
  • Skylights: $300 to $800. Cracked covers and failed seals.

What pushes the price up or down

  • How far the damage has spread. A leak caught early is a patch. A leak left through a winter can mean new decking and a ceiling repair inside.
  • The roofing material. Asphalt shingles are on the low end. Metal, slate and tile cost more to repair.
  • How steep and high the roof is. This Old House notes that roofs steeper than an 8/12 pitch need extra safety work, which can add $1,000 to $3,000.
  • Labor and timing. In the same guide, 65% of homeowners surveyed said labor was the biggest share of their roofing bill, and repairs cost more in the busy season from late spring to early fall. After a big Omaha hailstorm, demand jumps and schedules fill.
  • Emergency calls. Same-day or after-hours work can add several hundred dollars. See emergency roof repair if water is coming in now.
  • Extras. An inspection ($100 to $600 when you are not sure what is wrong), a permit if the job needs one ($75 to $250 is typical nationally), and gutter repairs if the fascia is involved.

Why Omaha roofs need repairs

Omaha's weather is hard on roofs from both directions. Spring and summer storms bring hail and high winds that crack, bruise and lift shingles. Winter brings freeze and thaw, and the ice dams that push meltwater under shingles at the eaves. Most repairs here trace back to one of those two seasons. Our storm damage page and roof leak page explain each one.

How to tell whether your quote is fair

  • Get it in writing and itemized. What is being repaired, the materials, labor and total. For insured storm work, Nebraska law requires the contractor to give you and your insurer an itemized breakdown before starting (Neb. Rev. Stat. 44-8606).
  • Compare two or three quotes for anything beyond a small patch, and compare the same scope, not just the bottom line.
  • Check the contractor's registration in the Nebraska Department of Labor contractor search, and ask for their insurance certificate.
  • Be wary of anyone offering to cover your insurance deductible. Nebraska law bars contractors from paying or rebating it to win the job (Neb. Rev. Stat. 44-8604).
  • Ask who pulls the permit, if the job needs one. For permitted work, the City of Omaha says you need an approved permit before construction starts (City of Omaha Planning Department). Bellevue, Papillion, La Vista and the other suburbs have their own building departments.

Can insurance pay for the repair?

If a storm caused the damage, your homeowners policy may cover it, minus your deductible. Wear and tear from age usually is not covered. For a small repair, the cost may be under your deductible, so it is worth getting the repair price before you file. Our hail damage claim guide walks through the claim step by step.
