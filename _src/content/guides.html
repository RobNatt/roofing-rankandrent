How we write these guides

These guides are written for homeowners in Omaha and the nearby suburbs. We keep them in plain English and avoid sales talk. Any cost or insurance figure links to the source it came from and says what year it is from, so you can check it yourself. When we work through numbers to show how something is calculated, we label it as an example so nobody mistakes it for a quote.

Omaha Roofing Experts is a referral service. We connect homeowners with independent local roofing contractors. We are not an insurance company, a lawyer or a public adjuster, and nothing here is legal or insurance advice. For questions about your own policy, talk to your agent or the Nebraska Department of Insurance.
